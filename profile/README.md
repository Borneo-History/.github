# Borneo History Resources

We are building a searchable digital archive of Borneo's historical documents — starting with the *British North Borneo Herald* (1881–1941), Colonial Office records, North Borneo Annual Reports, and related material.

Our work combines archival processing, OCR pipelines, semantic search, and public knowledge products.

- **Website:** [borneohistory.com](https://borneohistory.com)
- **Archive index:** [borneopedia.vercel.app](https://borneopedia.vercel.app)
- **Contact:** admin@borneohistory.com
